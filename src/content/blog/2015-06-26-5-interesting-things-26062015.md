<b><u>The artpiece of command line</u></b> - I'm a geek, I admit it and I like working from the command line (we love you Tom).  Both for small data science things (sum, average, uniq, etc.) and for checking up what is going on in my system (atop, ps, etc.). I fell I should keep this repository not more than a click a way.

<strong><u>Mattermost</u></strong> - mattermost is an open source alternative for Slack. In one of the organizations I worked introducing Slack changed the entire communication in the organization. People were sharing more of their ideas, thoughts, results and got involved into additional projects. Still some features made me feel inconvenient like preferring Slack over documentation, using external servers and hard to search \ backup. I therefore find it very exciting to have an open source alternative that will improve both products.

<a href="http://www.mattermost.org/">http://www.mattermost.org/</a>
