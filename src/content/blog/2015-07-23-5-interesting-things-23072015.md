<div><strong><u>Diversity in tech?</u></strong> This post describe a black woman’s experience in the Stanford Computer Science Major. As part of TOA events I went to Zalando's <a href="http://www.eventbrite.com/e/diversity-in-tech-panel-lunch-tickets-17658990516">diversity in tech panel</a> last week. So I got to think about this subject for a while both from my situation - foreign women in tech, not speaking the local language and from a wider point of view. Having women in Universities (and any other population) is a necessary condition (but not sufficient)  to having diversity in tech.</div>
