<div><strong><u>rrrepo</u></strong> -  at first glance it looked me like a checklist site and then I understood it is a repository of links. I tried to register, explore and create a site and I think they have many usability issues, some issue for example -</div>
<div>
<ul>
	<li>After registering I expected to get some mail confirming my registration and to automatically log into the site. It was unclear how to log into the site.</li>
	<li>Wanted to create a repo in the name which already exists, wanted to navigate to it but I was redircted to somewhere else.</li>
	<li>Exploring new repositories is not very clear.</li>
	<li>Privacy settings.</li>
	<li>When logged in, user is redirected to the news feed and cannot visit the home page or log out.</li>
</ul>
<div>I have a bit of dejavu to urli.st which was closed a while ago but with a nicer UI. It is currently on very very early stage and many improvements to be done.</div>
