<strong><u>Monopoly as Markov Chain</u></strong> - I guess I am developing a fetish to Markov chains. Although this model is not always realistic (so as in this case..) it is amazing what we can get out of it and the approximate simulations we can create. This post simulates a Monopoly game using Markov chains and does a very interesting job. Monopoly is linear in the sense that you must move according to the dice and the choices you make are limited (buy or don't buy). In contrast to backgammon where you also have strategy involved and therefore it is a better choice to model it with Markov chains.

<a title="http://koaning.io/monopoly-simulations.html Cmd+Click or tap to follow the link" href="http://koaning.io/monopoly-simulations.html" target="_blank">http://koaning.io/monopoly-simulations.html</a>
