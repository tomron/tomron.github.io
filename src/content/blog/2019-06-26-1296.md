<div><b><u>Google What-If-Tool (WIT)</u></b> - A nice tool by Google that was released few month ago. The terminology is actually a bit misleading and counterfactuals don't carry the meaning they have in causal inference. It is more like matching with two possible distance matrices - L1 and L2.</div>
