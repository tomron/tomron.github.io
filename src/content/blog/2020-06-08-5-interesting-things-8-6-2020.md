<p><strong><span style="text-decoration:underline;">DBScan practitioners guide</span></strong>&nbsp;- DBScan is a density-based clustering method. One important advantage&nbsp;comparing to K-means is DBScan's ability to identify noise and outliers. I feel that&nbsp; DBScan is often under-estimated. See this guide to learn more on how DBScan works, how to choose hyperparameters and more.</p>

<p><strong><span style="text-decoration:underline;">Dataset shift</span></strong>&nbsp;- dataset shift happens when the test set and the train set come from different distributions. There are multiple expressions of this phenomenon, such as covariate shift, concept shift, prior distribution shift. I believe that every data scientist working in the industry came across at least one of those manifestations. This post provides a very good introduction to the topic and useful links if you want to delve.</p>
