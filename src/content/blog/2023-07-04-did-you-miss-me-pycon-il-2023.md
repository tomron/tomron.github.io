<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I talked about working with missing data at <a href="https://2023.pycon.org.il/">PyCon IL</a>. We started with a bit of theory about mechanisms of missing data -</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MCAR - The fact that the data are missing is independent of the observed and unobserved data.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAR - The fact that the data are missing is systematically related to the observed but not the unobserved data.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MNAR - The fact that the data are missing is systematically related to the unobserved data.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And deep-dived into an almost real-world example that utilizes the Python ecosystem - pandas, scikit-learn, and missingno.</p>
