<p>Today, I heard Dafna Rosenblum’s (see Dafna’s blog <a href="https://dafir.medium.com/">here</a>) talk on the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/engineeringmanagersil/">EMIL</a> (Engineering Manager IL) meetup about “Team Health Check”. It was the first time I heard about the concept, so I read more about it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Spotify developed the team health check concept and introduced it in 2014 (<a href="https://engineering.atspotify.com/2014/09/squad-health-check-model/">here</a>).  About 6 months ago, Spotify published a new <a href="https://engineering.atspotify.com/2023/03/getting-more-from-your-team-health-checks/">post</a> about “Getting More from Your Team Health Checks”. The post focuses on improving the team experience in this workshop and suggests the following main ideas -</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Customize wisely</strong> - tailor the right questions and health checks that fit the team and the organization.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Dig Deep</strong> - Good facilitation is essential to enable more profound conversations. Good facilitation should help to step outside the team's day-to-day communication patterns and create a psychologically safe place (see <a href="https://dafir.medium.com/what-is-psychological-safety-and-servant-leadership-and-why-its-important-d6063f702615">here</a>) to raise issues. The post suggests a few strategies on how to do it.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Follow through</strong> - you should follow up and reiterate the topics that were raised in the team health check workshop. That can be in 1-1 meetings, scheduling required meetings, checking your priority or attention, etc.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I found a few online tools to help facilitate team health checks -&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><a href="https://teamhealthcheck.io/">teamhealthcheck.io</a> - “A free, anonymous, and super simple tool to run a version of the Spotify Health Check Survey. “</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Miro boards - There are multiple miro templates for team health checks - e.g., <a href="https://miro.com/miroverse/squad-health-check/">here</a>, <a href="https://miro.com/miroverse/team-health-check/">here</a>, and <a href="https://miro.com/miroverse/podteam-health-check/">here</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Another tip from Dafna to start meeting (and every meeting) is to start with a quick intake to break the ice and increase engagement. For example, ask the member to describe their mood using an emoji.</p>
