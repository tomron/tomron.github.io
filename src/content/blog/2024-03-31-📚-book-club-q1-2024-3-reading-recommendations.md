<p>This year, I track my book reading for the first time. I don't know if I'll keep doing it after this year or if I'll last until the end of the year, but for now, I'm all in. This helps me reflect on my reading and remember the books I enjoyed.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>STFU: The Power of Keeping Your Mouth Shut in an Endlessly Noisy World</strong>&nbsp;by Dan Lyons</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For long parts of this book, it was a paradox to me - why write 200+ pages to say we should all shut up - just do it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I listened to this book after also listening to <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Disrupted-My-Misadventure-Start-Up-Bubble-ebook/dp/B013CATZIC?crid=1CTIMHXMH9G8X&amp;dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.iSPkwz6TFaTtotq-x8i7ge3-Bmcc9Rh7352AKNfeS8YJJ5ZQIJgSQsU2E7R6Sx4z-bFPc8N91SnDwWlJIMfD3einmacYb0ECcP8QOPIdutpzB5_Jflrpi580kUEF-lYdOBk7keA-TpUawoQwrraVB09q8yfhRzHGzK42E8dqIG04tEHmiQ22s0COxrsR5KpgQUWNcN0X_A0YNoMe6XLg-vnwGOCgbuTjLh0PZBopZb8.xChM5imxhgfytkwDtR-h4U0aV6mMBukXJqdMUcMdIQg&amp;dib_tag=se&amp;keywords=disrupted&amp;qid=1711094290&amp;s=digital-text&amp;sprefix=disturbe,digital-text,219&amp;sr=1-1">Disrupted</a> by Lyons. I like his style and narration. When listening to Disrupted, I thought that it probably damaged his employability, which turned out to be true, as he discussed in STFU.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The book can sometimes be extreme or refer to an extreme crowd (50 out of 50 in the Talkaholics test). However, I liked the book because it discusses many aspects of our lives - friends and family, work, etc.- and a few things I can immediately adapt. For example, I lowered my cell phone usage near my kids and hope to stick with it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In one of the last chapters, he mentions <a href="https://www.audible.com/pd/Never-Split-the-Difference-Audiobook/1473575346">Never Split the Difference</a> by Chris Voss and Tahl Raz, which I also recently listened&nbsp;to. I listened to Never Split the Difference&nbsp;after a friend told me that she was starting to reread this book, and after listening to it, I completely understood why she wanted to reiterate it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I read the book to improve my negotiation skills. I'm not sure there is an immediate effect, but as the two books pointed out - I talk less and actively listen more. So I try to pause before I answer, be succinct and hum, and let the other person talk.</p>
