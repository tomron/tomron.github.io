<p><strong><u><mark style="background-color:#fff" class="has-inline-color">PgAI</mark></u></strong> - LLMs have been part of everyday life already for a while. One aspect I think has not been explored well so far is using them as part of ETL. The implementations I have seen so far don't take advantage of batch APIs and are not standardized to enable the easy replacement of a model. Having said that, I believe those hurdles will be overcome soon.</p>

<p><strong><u>Simplify Your Tech Stack: Use PostgreSQL for Everything</u></strong> - Two widespread tensions, especially in startups, are build vs. buy conflicts and using specialized products or technologies (e.g., different databases) that are top of the breed but not many people can use and maintain vs. more common technology that more people can maintain but can have performance drawbacks or other limitations. Mainly working in startups, I usually prefer to use standard technology to run faster, knowing that the product, focus, and priorities often change. With that being said, I acknowledge that early adoption of new technologies can be life-changing for a startup, but figuring out what to bet on is hard.</p>
