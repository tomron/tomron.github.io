<p>I finished listening to “<a href="https://www.amazon.com/Great-CEO-Within-Tactical-Building/dp/B08V1421ZJ">The Great CEO Within</a>” by Matt Mochary and Alex MacCaw. A few thoughts:<br><br>1️⃣ A tactical cheat sheet<br>I view the book as a tactical cheat sheet: short, practical chapters you can skim for ideas. It’s great for quick exposure, and most chapters include references for deeper dives. For me, this book has excellent value for time.<br><br>2️⃣ Revisit in the LLM era<br>Two well-known ideas in the book are Getting Things Done and Inbox Zero.<br>Inbox-zero and productivity advice hit differently today. With LLMs helping triage emails, summarize threads, and highlight what actually matters, the principles remain the same - but the execution is far more automated.<br><br>3️⃣ Optimizing meetings<br>TL;DR: come prepared, use written communication in advance, and don’t deviate from the planned agenda.<br>The authors suggest holding all meetings, including 1:1s, on the same day. From my experience, for 1:1s that go beyond status updates and require real attention (e.g., feedback), stacking too many of them on the same day can be overwhelming for most people.<br><br>4️⃣ “The first thing to optimize is yourself”<br>One of my favorite quotes from the book. It emphasizes founders’ and leaders’ health, mental and physical, something that has historically been overlooked. A good reminder that sustainable leadership starts with managing your own energy.<br><br>The book also mentions principles of conscious leadership: listening to feedback and acting on it, and not being afraid to make (and admit) mistakes.<br>This week, I also read a blog titled “<a href="https://kevingoldsmith.substack.com/p/reflection-is-a-leadership-skill">Reflection is a Crucial Leadership Skill</a>”, which made these ideas more actionable and down-to-earth.<br><br>What should I read next?</p>
