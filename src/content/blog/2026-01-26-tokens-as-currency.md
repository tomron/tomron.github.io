<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Half-baked thought: Tokens will become currency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right now, the direction is obvious - more money buys more tokens.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But what if, in the near future, tokens themselves become a medium of exchange?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Consider this:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Microsoft is allocating tokens to support the maintenance of open-source projects.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Companies granting tokens in exchange for using their tools or infrastructure.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Open-source maintainers receiving donations in tokens instead of (or alongside) cash.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Platforms enabling distributed token usage across multiple accounts, almost like a modern SETI@home</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Gift cards for Anthropic.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In other words, tokens are not just consumption units, but are tradable, transferable assets within an ecosystem.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Of course, this is far from trivial. Privacy, security, incentive alignment, and implementation complexity are all major hurdles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But if I had to place one slightly outrageous bet for 2026, it would be movement in this direction.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Are we ready to start thinking of tokens as something you can “round up and donate”?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
